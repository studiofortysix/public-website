Check the data, define the measures, repair the tracking or make the reporting useful. Each engagement starts with a question and a clear scope.

## GA4 & GTM audits

Check what you can trust

When analytics, advertising and customer records tell different stories, another report rarely resolves the problem. I review the measurement behind the numbers, test the agreed customer journeys and explain what the evidence can support.

**A useful fit:** Marketing managers, business owners and agency teams who need an independent view of an existing setup.

[Explore GA4 & GTM audits](/ga4-gtm-audit/)

## Conversion tracking

Measure the action that matters

A submit-button click is different from an enquiry received. A completion page is different from a confirmed customer. I help define the right signals, implement the agreed tracking and check that those signals behave as intended.

**A useful fit:** Organisations and agencies launching a website, repairing unreliable tracking or adding an important customer journey.

[Explore conversion tracking](/conversion-tracking/)

## Dashboards & reporting

Make the report useful

A useful dashboard starts with the decisions it needs to support. I build and improve reporting around the questions your marketing team, management or board actually asks, then make the definitions and limitations clear.

**A useful fit:** Marketing teams, organisational leaders and agencies who need a consistent view of performance across agreed data sources.

[Explore dashboards & reporting](/dashboards-reporting/)

## Measurement strategy

Define what success means

If every platform has its own version of success, it is difficult to know what marketing is contributing. I help your team define useful measures, map the evidence across systems and decide what needs to change to answer the important questions.

**A useful fit:** Organisations with several marketing channels, a CRM or a longer sales process, and teams planning a website or reporting change.

[Explore measurement strategy](/measurement-strategy/)

## Analytics support for agencies

Add specialist delivery capacity

When a client needs tracking, an audit or reporting expertise, you do not always need a permanent analytics hire. I work with agency teams on defined projects and ongoing support, from scoping the problem through to implementation, validation and explanation.

**A useful fit:** Digital, creative, development and media agencies that need senior measurement support for their clients.

[Explore analytics support for agencies](/agency-analytics-support/)

## Public sector & regulatory analytics

Make website reporting defensible

Website activity can help answer useful questions about information, journeys and service use. I help teams define those questions, improve the measurement and build reporting that makes its meaning and limitations clear.

**A useful fit:** Digital, communications and reporting teams in public organisations and professional regulators, including work delivered through an agency partner.

[Explore public sector & regulatory analytics](/public-sector-analytics/)

## How the services fit together.

**An audit** checks whether your current measurement is dependable. **A measurement strategy** defines what you should measure and where the evidence needs to come from.

**Conversion tracking** implements and validates agreed website signals. **Dashboards and reporting** make the available evidence useful to the people making decisions.

**Agency support** is a way to deliver those capabilities alongside your team. **Public sector and regulatory support** applies them to website reporting, service use and organisational requirements.

If you are unsure which one you need, describe the problem. We can establish a sensible starting point before agreeing any work.
