**Know which numbers you can trust before you use them to make a decision.**

When analytics, advertising and customer records tell different stories, another report rarely resolves the problem. I review the measurement behind the numbers, test the agreed customer journeys and explain what the evidence can support.

[Discuss an analytics audit](/contact/)

## Does this sound familiar?

- Your reports show more conversions than the enquiries or customers your team recognises.
- A website has changed, but nobody is sure whether the tracking still measures the right actions.
- Your GA4 property or GTM container has accumulated events and tags with unclear purposes.

## What you receive.

Marketing managers, business owners and agency teams who need an independent view of an existing setup.

### A measurement brief

The questions you need answered, the systems in scope and your definitions of an enquiry, conversion or customer.

### A review of the setup

An agreed review of GA4, Google Tag Manager and relevant website journeys, with advertising or CRM checks where included.

### Evidence from selected journeys

Documented checks that distinguish a click, an attempted submission and a confirmed action.

### Findings in priority order

What is unreliable, why it matters and which issues deserve attention first.

### A practical next-step plan

Recommendations your team or developer can use, with an explanation of open questions and limitations.

The audit is a defined diagnostic engagement. Repairs can be commissioned afterwards or handed to your developer. Any wider review of campaign profitability or business systems is agreed separately.

## A useful decision at the end of the audit

You should be able to decide which reports are useful now, which decisions need better evidence and what to fix first. I connect technical findings to their consequences for enquiries, customers and reporting, so the work can be discussed with both developers and management.

## How the work proceeds.

1. **Agree the questions**: We confirm the business questions, key journeys, access and the boundaries of the review.
2. **Inspect and test**: I inspect the configuration and test the agreed journeys, documenting the evidence and any gaps.
3. **Work through the findings**: You receive a written report and a discussion of the priorities and implementation options.

## Before we start.

### Is this an automated GA4 audit?

Automated checks can help identify issues, but the work also considers your business definitions, website behaviour and the evidence behind your reports. The scope is agreed around the decisions you need to make.

### Will you fix the issues as part of the audit?

The diagnostic scope and any implementation work are agreed explicitly. You can use the findings with your existing developer or ask me to scope the repairs.

### What access do you need?

Usually access to the relevant GA4 property and GTM container, plus the website journeys in scope. Advertising, CRM or developer access may also be useful. We confirm what is needed before starting.

### How much does an audit cost?

I quote after confirming the websites, properties, journeys and systems involved. You receive a defined scope and fee before the work begins.
