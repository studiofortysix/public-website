**Add experienced measurement support while keeping a clear client relationship.**

When a client needs tracking, an audit or reporting expertise, you do not always need a permanent analytics hire. I work with agency teams on defined projects and ongoing support, from scoping the problem through to implementation, validation and explanation.

[Discuss an agency project](/contact/)

## Does this sound familiar?

- Your developers can implement a brief, but need someone to define and validate the measurement.
- A client has conflicting reports and needs a clear explanation before making a decision.
- Your team needs additional analytics capacity across several projects without an undefined support commitment.

## What you receive.

Digital, creative, development and media agencies that need senior measurement support for their clients.

### Scoping and diagnosis

A practical assessment of the measurement problem and the work needed.

### Technical collaboration

GA4/GTM configuration, developer briefs, selected journey testing and documented findings.

### Client-ready explanation

Reports and recommendations your team can use in the client conversation.

### A clear working arrangement

Agreement on scope, communication, client contact, deliverables and capacity before starting.

### Continuity through documentation

A record of definitions, configuration, tests and dependencies for the next person working on the account.

White-label, joint delivery and ongoing capacity are options to agree, rather than assumptions. Availability, response expectations and additional work are confirmed for each arrangement.

## Specialist judgement your team can use

You work directly with me. I can contribute behind the scenes or alongside your team, depending on the arrangement. The aim is dependable measurement delivery and a clear handover, with your agency retaining the agreed commercial lead.

## How the work proceeds.

1. **Brief the engagement**: We establish the client problem, deliverables, working relationships and access.
2. **Deliver within your workflow**: I work with the relevant account, development and marketing people on the agreed scope.
3. **Explain and hand over**: Your team receives the evidence, implementation notes and a clear view of the next decisions.

## Before we start.

### Can you work under our agency brand?

A behind-the-scenes or white-label arrangement can be agreed. We confirm how communication, attribution of work and client contact should operate before the engagement starts.

### Can we bring you into a client meeting?

Yes, where it helps define the problem or explain the findings. We agree the role and communication approach with your team.

### Do you provide one-off and ongoing support?

Both are possible. A project has a defined scope; ongoing work needs agreed capacity, priorities and deliverables.

### Will you take over campaign management?

The core offer is measurement, analytics and reporting. Campaign delivery and wider agency responsibilities stay with the people you appoint.
