**Measure accepted enquiries and completed actions with tracking your team can understand.**

A submit-button click is different from an enquiry received. A completion page is different from a confirmed customer. I help define the right signals, implement the agreed tracking and check that those signals behave as intended.

## Does this sound familiar?

- Forms and advertising platforms report conversions that cannot be reconciled with real enquiries.
- A new website, portal or campaign needs tracking designed and checked before launch.
- The same action is counted repeatedly, or a useful journey disappears between systems.

## What you receive.

Organisations and agencies launching a website, repairing unreliable tracking or adding an important customer journey.

### Clear event definitions

A record of what each event means, when it should occur and how it relates to the business action.

### GA4 and GTM implementation

The agreed tags, triggers and variables, with developer requirements where website changes are needed.

### Meaningful conversion signals

Selected form, lead, purchase or portal actions, based on the scope and the evidence available.

### Validation and handover

Documented checks for agreed success and failure scenarios, together with implementation notes.

### Source capture where useful

Requirements for preserving known campaign or referral information in the relevant system, where included.

The work covers the tracking implementation we agree. Website development, payment-system changes and wider CRM integration are scoped where needed. Historical data cannot be recreated simply by fixing future collection.

## Give reporting a more dependable foundation

Better tracking helps your team distinguish activity from meaningful actions and investigate discrepancies with evidence. Where website data needs to connect to a CRM or operational record, I make the handover requirements clear rather than treating a browser event as the final business outcome.

## How the work proceeds.

1. **Define the action**: We agree what a successful action means, where confirmation comes from and which tools are involved.
2. **Implement with your team**: I configure the tracking and work with your developer on any required website events or data.
3. **Validate and document**: We check the agreed scenarios and record what has been implemented, what was tested and any remaining dependencies.

## Before we start.

### Can you work with my current developer?

Yes. I can define the events or data needed, configure the tracking and work through validation with your existing developer or agency.

### Can you track enquiries rather than button clicks?

Where the website exposes a reliable success signal, tracking can be based on an accepted submission. We first establish how the form or system confirms success.

### Will GA4 exactly match my CRM?

The systems can count different things and may have gaps caused by consent, devices, timing or offline activity. I help define each measure and investigate differences. Exact agreement is not a universal expectation.

### Do you offer server-side tracking?

The first step is to establish whether it is useful for your measurement problem. Any server-side architecture or external integration needs a separately agreed scope and delivery approach.
